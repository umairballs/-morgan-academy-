Morgan Academy Arcade

Real, well-known browser games made by independent developers and released as open source. Every game is stored on this site, so nothing loads from outside game websites.

Credits & licenses

These games are the original work of their creators and are shared here under their open-source licenses. Changes made for this site: analytics, ads and social-media widgets were removed, and fonts and libraries are served locally. Each game's full license file is included in its folder.
